How to Find Your Tickets

Updated: 16 Jan 2026 • 3 min read

Finding your tickets is easy with Tanderrum. Whether you purchased them recently or months ago, your tickets are always accessible through your account or email.

Access Your Tickets Online

  • Log into your Tanderrum account and go to "My Tickets"
  • Check your email for the confirmation message with ticket links
  • Use the mobile app to view and manage your tickets on the go
  • Download PDF tickets directly from your account dashboard

All tickets purchased through Tanderrum are stored securely in your account. You can access them anytime before the event, even if you lose the original confirmation email.

Troubleshooting Tips

  • Ensure you're logged into the correct email account
  • Check your spam or junk folder for confirmation emails
  • Verify your purchase was completed successfully
  • Contact support if you can't find your tickets after 24 hours

For events requiring printed tickets, you can download and print your tickets from your account or show the digital version on your mobile device at the venue entrance.
