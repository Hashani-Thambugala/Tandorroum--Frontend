FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Will I be charged for the failed transaction?

No, you won't be charged for failed transactions. Funds are only deducted when payment is successful.

How long does it take for funds to be released?

Pending authorizations are typically released within 1-3 business days, depending on your bank.

Can I try with a different payment method?

Yes, you can use a different credit/debit card, PayPal, or other available payment methods.

Are my tickets reserved during payment attempts?

Tickets are temporarily held for 10 minutes during the payment process to prevent them from being sold to others.
