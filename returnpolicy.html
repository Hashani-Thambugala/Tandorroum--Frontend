Event Cancellations

Full refund for canceled events:

  • Auto-processed to original payment
  • Email notification sent
  • 5-10 business day processing

Event Postponements

If event is rescheduled:

  • Tickets remain valid for new date
  • Refund within 7 days of announcement
  • Submit request via your account

Service fees are non-refundable unless event is canceled by organizer.
